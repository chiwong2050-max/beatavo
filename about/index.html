About Beatavo

Beatavo is a free, family-safe place to watch cartoon dance videos and play along. It's made to be simple, quick and kind to kids — nothing to sign up for, nothing to install unless you want to.

What it is

Beatavo gathers fun cartoon music videos and plays them back to back so you can keep dancing. There's a home page to browse, a Kids space with extra-big buttons, a daily "Dance of the day", a gentle dance challenge, and playlists and favourites you can build yourself.

Family-safe by design

We pick every video for a family audience, and the dedicated Kids page keeps things simple: bigger buttons, larger text and no search box. A grown-ups-only lock helps keep little ones in the Kids area.

No accounts, no ads

There is nothing to sign up for. Beatavo has no advertising and runs no ad scripts or ad networks. Your favourites, playlists and history are stored only on your own device — see our Privacy page for the short version.

How the videos work

Videos are hosted on YouTube and play through its embedded player using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. The player only loads after you tap play, so browsing stays light and fast.

How to reach us

Beatavo is supported by people who enjoy it. If you'd like to say thanks, you can buy me a coffee — it's completely optional and always will be.
