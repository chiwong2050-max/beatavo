Privacy

Beatavo is a simple, family-safe place to watch cartoon dance videos. Here is the whole story — it's short.

No accounts

There are no sign-ups, logins or profiles. We don't ask for your name, email or any personal information.

No cookies of our own

Beatavo does not set its own cookies and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers. The app stores a small amount of data on your device (a cached copy of the app so it loads quickly) using your browser's service worker and local storage. This stays on your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Videos are hosted on YouTube

All videos play through YouTube's embedded player. We use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain, and the player is only loaded after you tap play. Even so, when a video plays YouTube (Google) may set its own cookies and collect data (such as your IP address and playback activity) under its own policies. We don't control that data.

  • YouTube's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google's how Google uses information page: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children's privacy

Beatavo is designed to be family-safe and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because we collect nothing ourselves, there is nothing for us to store, sell or share.

Third-party links

Our footer links to Buy Me a Coffee for optional support. That site has its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach out through the support link in the footer.
